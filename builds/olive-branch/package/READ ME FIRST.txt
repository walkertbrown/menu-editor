================================================================
  THE OLIVE BRANCH CAFÉ — YOUR EDITABLE MENU
================================================================

Thanks for taking a look! This is your menu — and now you can edit
it yourself, anytime, with no special software.

----------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO OPEN IT
----------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click the file:

    Olive Branch Cafe Menu Editor.html

It opens in your web browser (Chrome works best). That's it —
nothing to install.

----------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO EDIT
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Click any item name, price, or description to change the words.
• Use the tabs at the top to switch between the dine-in menu pages,
  the cover, the back, and the to-go trifold.
• Hover over an item to get a "grip" handle (drag to reorder) and
  an "x" (removes it to the Saved Items list on the left).
• Add something new: click "+ New item" on the left, type the name
  and price, then drag it onto the menu where you want it.

When you like how it looks, click  SAVE.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SAVING & BACKUP — YOU WON'T LOSE YOUR WORK
----------------------------------------------------------------
Every time you hit SAVE, your menu is kept in THREE places:

  1. In this browser (instant).
  2. Backed up to our private server automatically — you'll see
     "Backed up ✓" in the top corner.
  3. Any time you like, click "Download backup" to save a copy
     file you keep yourself.

If you ever edit while your internet is down, no problem — it
saves on your computer and backs up automatically the next time
you're online.

Got a new computer? Open the file and click "Restore from server"
to pull your latest menu back down.

----------------------------------------------------------------
PRINTING / PDF
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Click "Save PDF" to make a print-ready PDF of the page you're on.
• "Switch to B&W" gives you a black-and-white version for the
  office copier.
• The to-go trifold prints on regular letter paper, double-sided.

----------------------------------------------------------------
A FEW NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
• Keep this file somewhere easy to find (your Desktop is great).
• "Reset" puts the menu back to the original — it will ask first.
• Questions, or something not looking right? Just reach out to
  Walker and I'll take care of it.

Enjoy!
