Our Story

Founded in 1997 by Russell Autry — Rusty to his friends and customers — The Olive Branch Café is THE gourmet pizza restaurant in Marrero and Algiers. Rusty has plenty of restaurant experience and it shows: he's always at one of his stores making sure customers are satisfied, keeping his employees on their toes as they work hard to keep the business running smoothly day after day.

At The Olive Branch Café we use only the highest-quality cheeses and the freshest ingredients possible. We bring in the finest Spanish olives and Italian olive oil because our customers deserve the best. Fresh vegetables arrive daily so your pizza, salad, sub, or pasta is perfect every time. Our dough is made in house every day and nothing is prepared until you order it, so we can make everything just the way you like it — even our signature Caesar dressing is made almost daily from scratch.

The Olive Branch Café has proudly served fresh, delicious Italian cuisine for 29 years.
